# Supported reproduction levels and missing inputs

| Level | Meaning | This package |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Numerical reconstruction from included project outcome/count/ratio projections | Supported for controlled comparisons, four-arm interactions, local erasure, corrected replay conditional ranges, metric labels, exploratory KV and selected figures calculated from observations (frozen anchors alone do not receive coverage credit). The 28 portable tests plus full reconstruction/artist checks define the supported suite. Additional held-out, prospective and Stage4C arithmetic is described in claim_coverage.md. |
| 2 | Reproduction requiring public upstream datasets | Not promised. Upstream acquisition links are attribution, not a complete simulation pipeline. Private simulator dependencies, exact filtered files and licensing/provenance gaps remain. |
| 3 | Historical outputs with incompletely reconstructable execution environments | Frozen diagnostic generation, prospective/held-out extensions, benchmark predictive tests, some opportunity anchors and implementation-state lineage. Their supported summaries/limitations are retained, but no claim of independent raw execution reproduction. |
| 4 | External real-system validation | Not established. No valid real-vLLM confirmatory treatment-effect estimate. |

The original full legacy test suite is not included or claimed to pass. It had missing benchmark Parquet inputs; copying such tests without their dependencies would misrepresent the release. Portable tests are about this package's supported reconstruction surface.

Missing/withheld inputs: raw or reconstructive third-party traces; source row ranges and request identities; historical private simulator snapshots and services; exact filtered BurstGPT bytes; some cloned-state/mutation-isolation attestations; original per-window replay decision counts; legacy benchmark Parquets and complete independent-group predictive evaluation inputs. The three erasure pairs and 38 eligible-pair inventory are not a new broad representativeness claim. The 1,512 source-record count and disjointness were checked privately against historical inputs; the snapshot cannot independently re-establish their identities.

Observed zero interactions and aggregate nulls are retained, not recast as equivalence. Held-out transfer is weak or source-dependent. Prospective actionability improvement remains inconclusive; perfect AUROC has one independent test group. Frozen aggregate evidence is identified separately in `provenance/claim_register.json`. Shared Azure/BurstGPT records preclude claims of independence from the related whole-policy/headroom manuscript.

The source development commit identifies the material from which projections were made. It is not an executable historical environment, and private input hashes are identification evidence rather than download promises. Frozen figure annotations about older replications remain reference-only. Public reconstruction figures are new views of the same observations, not byte-identical replacements for journal artwork.
